About the girl behind the pages

Four things about me

Hi, hello, namaste

A bubbly, filmy, lantern loving girl

I'm Rashmi. I'm the girl who cries at the right scenes, laughs too loud at the wrong ones, and replays every conversation like a movie I can still edit.

I fell in love with stories long before I knew how to write my own, hiding behind the sofa and watching my mother's 8 p.m. serials. Somewhere along the way those stories started living inside me. Now they finally have a home.

This little corner of the internet is where my heart lives, out loud.

Why I write

Because on paper, the ending is mine

Writing lets me escape the hard reality. It lets me live in my delusions, and it lets me finish the stories that were left incomplete, or miserable, or simply too harsh for me.

In real life I cannot rewrite a goodbye. On paper I can give it a second chance, a softer word, a better sunset. I can hold the kite and the string at the same time.

I love my delusions, and finally, I have a place to pour them out.

A confession

I bought over a hundred notebooks and kept none

Since I was a child, I have bought more than 100 aesthetic notebooks and diaries. Pretty covers, pastel pages, little ribbons. And every single time I finished writing my thoughts and my stories in them, I tore all the pages out.

I felt insecure, and honestly, I still don't know what for. Even now, one article takes me a long, long time. I write, I doubt, I rewrite, I hide it, I come back.

This website is the first diary I am not tearing.

A love letter to my own head

I love my delusions, and I'm keeping them

People told me to grow up and be realistic. So I made my realism a little theatrical. I call every flutter a prophecy, I believe in slow love and steady people, and I still think a sky lantern can carry a problem away.

Every piece here took weeks of my heart. If you are reading one, you are holding a page I almost tore out, and didn't.

I love me. I love my thoughts. I love being filmy.
