Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

This is a personal writing website run by Rashmi Devkota at rashmidevkota.github.io/pages-i-never-tore. This page explains what information the site collects and how it is used.

What you share with me

When you answer a question, leave a sticky note on the Note Wall, or send a whisper about a story, the words you type are saved so other visitors can read them. These are anonymous. The site does not ask for your name, email or account, so please don't type personal details into them.

If you use the contact form, the name, reply address and message you choose to give are sent privately to me and are not shown on the site.

Where it is stored

Notes, whispers and messages are stored with Google Firebase. Your browser also keeps a few small items on your own device, such as which notes you wrote and how many lanterns you released, so the page can show them back to you. You can clear these at any time by clearing your browser data.

Advertising and cookies

This site may show ads through Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can turn off personalized advertising at Google Ads Settings, and learn more at How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

The site may also include links to shops. If you buy something through an affiliate link, I may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you.

Analytics

I may use simple visitor statistics to understand which stories people read. These do not identify you personally.

Removing something you posted

If you posted a note or whisper and want it removed, use the contact page and tell me what it said. I will delete it.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent through the contact page.
